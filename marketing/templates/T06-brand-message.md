# T06 — Brand Message

## Purpose
Institutional template for positioning, announcements, launches and direct brand CTAs.

## Anatomy
1. Logo.
2. Large 1–2 line brand statement.
3. Short supporting copy.
4. Three value pillars.
5. Optional pill-shaped CTA.
6. Natural/origin imagery on the lower/right area with restrained botanical line art.

## Best for
Company positioning, “Premium Brazilian Superfoods”, new capability announcements, campaign openers/closers and commercial calls to action.

## CTA
Primary:
- Talk to Biohacker Foods

Alternatives:
- Discover our ingredients
- Request specifications
