# T02 — Educational Comparison

## Purpose
Explain a meaningful technical difference without looking like a dense infographic.

## Anatomy
1. Logo.
2. Large comparison headline: “A vs B”.
3. One short framing sentence.
4. Two balanced visual columns.
5. Three comparison rows with a central icon/category.
6. Thin botanical-gold rules and dividers.

## Copy limits
- Headline: max 6 words.
- Option intro: max 22 words per side.
- Each comparison point: max 18 words per side.

## Best for
Freeze-Dried vs Spray-Dried, Organic vs Conventional, Powder vs Pulp, carrier-free vs carrier-based, process education and format selection.

## Guardrail
Only compare attributes supported by current technical knowledge. Avoid absolute superiority claims unless documented.

## CTA
- Request specifications
- Ask which format fits your formulation
