# T04 — Origin & Sourcing

## Purpose
Communicate Brazilian / Latin American provenance, sourcing capability and international reach.

## Anatomy
1. Logo.
2. Large origin-led headline.
3. Short sourcing statement.
4. Three trust pillars: origin / quality / supply.
5. Landscape, harvest or ingredient-origin imagery.
6. Optional subtle map and route arcs in botanical gold.

## Image direction
Brazilian landscapes, Amazon or fruit-growing context when accurate, raw fruit, crates, leaves and supply-chain atmosphere. Never imply a specific farm, certification or traceability claim without evidence.

## Best for
Brazil-to-world positioning, sourcing stories, export narratives and origin education.
