# T03 — Application Spotlight

## Purpose
Show how an ingredient translates into a real food, beverage or wellness application.

## Anatomy
1. Logo.
2. Large application-led headline.
3. Short practical descriptor.
4. Three use/application chips.
5. Large finished-product photograph.
6. Secondary ingredient visual to connect raw ingredient with final application.

## Image direction
Smoothies, bowls, beverages, yogurt, functional blends, bakery or other credible application. Premium food styling, realistic texture, no generic fitness imagery.

## Best for
Ingredient inspiration, formulation ideas, customer-use education and visual variety in the feed.

- Discuss your formulation
