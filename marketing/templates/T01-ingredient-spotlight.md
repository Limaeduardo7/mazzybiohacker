# T01 — Ingredient Spotlight

## Purpose
Hero template for a single ingredient, fruit, powder, pulp or product family.

## Anatomy
1. Biohacker Foods logo, upper-left.
2. Optional small kicker/category.
3. Large 1–2 line ingredient name.
4. One short descriptor.
5. Three compact feature blocks.
6. Large premium ingredient photograph on the lower/right side.
7. Optional subtle botanical gold arc / leaf motif.

## Copy limits
- Headline: max 3–4 words.
- Descriptor: max 10 words.
- Feature labels: max 2–3 words each.
- Avoid paragraph copy on the cover.

## Image direction
Macro food photography, visible texture, powder/pulp/fruit in ceramic or natural materials, clean cream surface, green botanical framing, natural editorial light.

## Best for
Açaí Powder, Acerola, Pitaya, Maqui, Banana, Raspberry, Blueberry, fruit pulps and new ingredient introductions.

## CTA
Prefer caption CTA rather than a large button:
- Discover our ingredients
- Request specifications
- Ask about wholesale availability
