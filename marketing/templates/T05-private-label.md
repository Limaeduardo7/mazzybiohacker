# T05 — Private Label

## Purpose
Present packaging, custom formats and private-label capability with a premium commercial feel.

## Anatomy
1. Small template/category label.
2. Large “Private Label” or product-format headline.
3. Short commercial descriptor.
4. Three capability icons: format / branding / supply.
5. Pouch, jar or other packaging hero mockup.
6. Ingredient and fruit styling around the packaging.

## Packaging rule
Mockups must use neutral placeholder text until actual packaging, regulatory copy and brand rights are approved.

## Best for
Private label, custom packaging, branded powders, retail concepts and product-development conversations.
